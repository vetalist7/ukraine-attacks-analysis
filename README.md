# ukraine-attacks-analysis
Data analysis of missile and drone attacks in Ukraine (2022–2025)

## 📊 Key Findings

### 1. Growth of Drone Warfare
Drone attacks show a strong and sustained increase over time, becoming the dominant form of long-range strikes by 2025–2026.

---

### 2. Shift in Attack Composition
The share of drone attacks has increased significantly, while missile usage has not grown at the same rate.

---

### 3. Ballistic Missile Escalation
Ballistic missile usage increases notably from late 2024, with large-scale strike events occurring in 2025.

---

### 4. Cruise Missile Variability
Cruise missile usage remains inconsistent and does not show a clear long-term trend.

---

### 5. Weak Seasonality vs Strong Trend
While some seasonal patterns are visible, they are secondary to the overall upward trend in attack intensity.

---

### 6. Increasing Scale of Attacks
The largest attack events are concentrated in later periods and are primarily driven by drone usage.
